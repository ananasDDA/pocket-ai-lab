How fast does Gemma 4 E2B (GGUF, Vision) run on iPhone?

On an iPhone Air (A19, iOS 26.6.1), Gemma 4 E2B (GGUF, Vision) generated 35.0 tokens per second in Pocket AI Lab 1.1.0, averaged over 3 runs of 200 tokens (± 2.3).

On the standard llama-bench style probe it read a 512-token prompt at about 708 tok/s and generated 128 tokens at 32.8 tok/s. Cold load took 2.6 s and the process peaked at 1,490 MB.

How much memory and storage does Gemma 4 E2B (GGUF, Vision) need?

The catalog budgets 5.0 GB of RAM and 4.1 GB of storage for the Q4_K_M build of Gemma 4 E2B (GGUF, Vision). The measured peak footprint on iPhone Air was 1,490 MB. The 5.0 GB figure is the memory budget the app requires to load the model with its context, while 1,490 MB is what our benchmark run actually used.

It runs on a memory-mapped backend, so the app checks it against the Metal working set rather than the stricter per-app limit. Weights are read from disk as needed, which keeps the measured footprint low.

Which iPhones can run Gemma 4 E2B (GGUF, Vision)?

Gemma 4 E2B (GGUF, Vision) runs on iPhones with 8 GB of RAM or more. On 8 GB phones it fits with little headroom, so long chats may make it reload.
