How fast does Phi-3.5 Mini run on iPhone?

On an iPhone Air (A19, iOS 26.6.1), Phi-3.5 Mini generated 23.3 tokens per second in Pocket AI Lab 1.0.1, averaged over 3 runs of 200 tokens (± 2.7).

On the standard llama-bench style probe it read a 512-token prompt at about 94 tok/s and generated 128 tokens at 14.7 tok/s. Cold load took 1.0 s and the process peaked at 3,167 MB. The phone heated up during this run and decode speed dropped 21% between repetitions, so on a cool phone it can be faster.

How much memory and storage does Phi-3.5 Mini need?

The catalog budgets 2.4 GB of RAM and 2.3 GB of storage for the 4bit build of Phi-3.5 Mini. The measured peak footprint on iPhone Air was 3,167 MB.

Its backend copies weights into the app's memory, so the app checks it against the per-app limit iOS enforces. On a 6 GB iPhone that limit is much lower than 6 GB.

What can Phi-3.5 Mini do in Pocket AI Lab?

It's a text model for chat, drafting, summaries and quick questions, fully offline once downloaded.

Open the Models tab in Pocket AI Lab and tap Phi-3.5 Mini. The app checks it against your phone's memory, downloads 2.3 GB from Hugging Face and it's ready for offline chat.
